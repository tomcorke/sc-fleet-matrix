# Domain glossary

- **Pledge**: An entry in a backer's RSI hangar.
- **Melt**: RSI's “reclaim” action; exchange a pledge for store credit equal to its melt value.
- **Melt value**: The amount of store credit RSI returns when a pledge is melted.
- **Store credit**: Account balance used to pay for pledges.
- **New money**: Cash paid at checkout rather than store credit.
- **Warbond**: An offer payable only with new money, usually at a discount.
- **Standard edition**: A ship's regular store offer, rather than its warbond edition.
- **MSRP**: The standard edition's listed price before tax.
- **Insurance tier**: A pledge or offer's insurance duration; LTI ranks above any number of months.
- **Upgrade (CCU)**: A Cross-Chassis Upgrade that changes a pledge to a more expensive ship while keeping its insurance.
- **Warbond upgrade**: A discounted, new-money-only upgrade to a more expensive ship.
- **Chain**: A sequence of upgrades that moves one pledge through intermediate ships.
- **Keep**: Planner group for owned pledges marked to remain unchanged.
- **Trade in**: Planner group for owned pledges available to melt or upgrade.
- **Want**: Planner entry for a desired ship and its minimum insurance tier.
- **Funding mode**: Planner preference for how to use store credit and new money.
